Algorithms

Hold the cube with white on the bottom and yellow on top (or match your own layout). x = rotate the whole cube like an R turn, M = middle slice turned like L. On OLL pictures, yellow means the sticker is already oriented. On PLL pictures, arrows show where pieces move. The pictures show the top of the cube from above, with the front at the bottom. Check any algorithm against a trusted source such as J Perm or SpeedSolving before you memorise it.

F2L — front-right slot

Six basic cases for pairing a corner and edge and inserting them into the front-right slot. The grid is the top of the cube seen from above, front at the bottom; the dashed cell is the slot the pair is heading for. White (the corner's other colour) marks the sticker facing outward or down.

Move notation

Each letter is a face. Turn it 90° clockwise, facing it directly.

R' = same face, other way. R2 = turn it twice.

Beginner method

1. White crossPlace the four white edges around the white centre, matching the side colours.
2. White cornersInsert the four white corners to finish the first layer.
3. Middle layerMove the four edge pieces into the middle layer.
4. Yellow crossMake a yellow cross on the top face.
5. Yellow faceOrient the last-layer corners so the whole top is yellow.
6. Position cornersSwap the last-layer corners into their correct places.
7. Position edgesCycle the last edges to finish the cube.
